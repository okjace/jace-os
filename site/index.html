Familiar from the first click

If you've used Windows, you already know how to use Jace OS. If you've used Arch, you'll find everything you love underneath.

🪟

Windows-style windows

Minimize, maximize and close on the right. Drag to the top to maximize, to a side to snap. Alt+Tab, Win+arrows and Alt+F4 all work.

🔲

A start menu on the Windows key

Pinned apps, every app and search as you type. Pin, hide, rename or add apps and websites, and choose where it opens and how big it is.

🏝️

A top bar you can make your own

Rounded islands inspired by niri. Turn modules on and off (Now Playing, weather, processor, Do not disturb, Keep awake…), move it to the bottom, pick a 12 or 24-hour clock.

⚙️

One Settings app for everything

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, displays, sound, wallpaper, mouse, keyboard shortcuts, power, users, apps, updates and virus protection, all in one place.

The little things

Small touches that make it nice to use every day.

📋

Clipboard history

Press Win+V for everything you copied, pictures included. Search it, pin things to keep them, and click to paste.

📅

Calendar on the clock

Click the clock (or press Win+N) for a calendar drop-down.

🔒

A lock screen worth looking at

Your blurred wallpaper, a big clock, your picture and name, media controls and power buttons.

🙂

Face unlock

Sign in and unlock by looking at your camera, like Windows Hello. Best with an infrared camera; your password always works too.

🐸

Your picture, your way

Pick a ready-made picture, build your own from colours and an emoji or your initials, or use a photo. Signed in to Jace? Your Jace name and photo come along.

⌨️

Every shortcut is yours

Change or turn off any keyboard shortcut, or add your own that open apps or run commands.

🖱️

Mouse and pointer

Pointer speed and acceleration, left-handed buttons, scroll direction and speed, and pointer themes and sizes.

🛍️

Get apps right in Settings

Search Jace Store, Flathub and the Arch repositories and install with one click. The AUR works too: yay is included.

📦

AppImages just work

Double-click one to open it. Right-click it and choose Install to add it to the start menu with its own icon.

Safe without trying

You never have to open a terminal to stay protected.

🛡️

Updates install themselves

Linux, security fixes, the Jace apps and Flatpaks update in the background. Jace OS tells you if one needs a restart or a sign-out.

🦠

Built-in virus protection

Real-time protection scans downloads and new files and quarantines threats, plus a daily scan and quick and full scans in Settings.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑

Accounts for everyone

Add people with their own password and picture, and remove accounts you don't need any more.

🌊

Wayland all the way

A modern Wayland desktop (labwc) with screen sharing, fractional scaling and portals, and XWayland for older apps.

Your Jace apps, already installed

Sign in once with your Jace account and you're in everywhere, Jace Browser included.

⛏

Jace Launcher

Minecraft with every version, mod loaders, modpacks, skins and capes.

💬

Jace Social

Friends, chats, servers and voice calls, with Minecraft built in.

🛍

Jace Store

Apps, games and Minecraft content, plus Flathub, the Arch repos and the AUR (yay is included).

Sign in with Jace

Sign in once in Settings and your Jace name and photo show on the top bar, the login screen and the lock screen. Jace Browser and the Jace apps use the same account.

What's Jace?

Updates install themselves

Jace OS checks every few hours while you're plugged in and installs everything quietly.

  • The newest Linux kernel and security fixes from Arch Linux
  • New versions of Jace Launcher, Jace Social, Jace Store and Jace Browser within hours of their release
  • Flatpak apps and virus signatures too
  • The two newest versions of everything stay cached, so a bad update can be undone
  • It tells you when an update needs a restart or a sign-out, with a button to do it
  • See and install updates yourself any time in Settings → Updates

Install Jace OS

You need a USB stick (8 GB or bigger) and an internet connection.

  1. Download the ISO

    Get the newest ISO from GitHub or Jace Store. If it comes in parts, join them first (the release notes show how).

  2. Write it to a USB stick

    Use balenaEtcher or Rufus on Windows or macOS, or dd on Linux. This erases the stick.

  3. Start your computer from the USB stick

    Open your computer's boot menu (often F12, F11 or Esc while it starts) and pick the USB stick. Choose Try Jace OS to use it straight from the USB stick without changing anything, or Install Jace OS.

  4. Install it

    The installer asks for your language, keyboard, the disk to use (with optional encryption), your name and a password. Then it installs Jace OS with the newest Jace apps and Jace Browser. Want to keep Windows too? Use Advanced install.

Already on Arch?

Add the Jace OS repository to /etc/pacman.conf, then install the desktop and apps and choose “Jace OS” on your login screen.

[jace-os]
SigLevel = Optional TrustAll
Server = https://github.com/okjace/jace-os/releases/download/repo
sudo pacman -Syu jace-os jace-launcher-bin jace-social-bin jace-store-bin jace-browser-bin yay-bin
